Privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026

This page explains what happens to the email address you enter on the portfolio site. That is the only personal data the site collects.

What is collected

  • Your email address.
  • The date and time you signed up, and the fact that you confirmed the signup, so the consent can be shown.

What it is used for

  • Occasional emails about my games and projects, and the download link for Rudy.
  • Nothing else. The address is never sold, rented or shared with anyone beyond the mailing service named below.

Why it is allowed

You give explicit consent by submitting the field and clicking the confirmation link in the email that follows (double opt-in). You can withdraw that consent at any time, without giving a reason, by using the unsubscribe link in every email or by writing to me.

Who processes it

The list is hosted by Kit (ConvertKit LLC, United States), which stores the addresses and sends the emails on my behalf. Kit processes the data under a data processing agreement with standard contractual clauses. See kit.com/privacy.

This site itself is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which keeps standard server logs including IP addresses for security and abuse prevention.

How long it is kept

Until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete it. Unsubscribing removes the address from the active list; a suppression record of the address may be kept so you are not mailed again by mistake.

Your rights

  • Ask what is stored about you, and get a copy of it.
  • Have it corrected or deleted.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be) or the authority in your own country.

Cookies and tracking

None. No analytics, no advertising pixels, no cookies are set by this site. The fonts and images are served from this domain.
